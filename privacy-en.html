Privacy Policy

Apology Dojo (“the Game”) is a browser game run by an individual. This policy explains how information is handled in the Game.

Operator

Operator: Sakamoto
Contact: info@shazaidojo.com

Information stored on your device

The name you enter, your settings and your best records are stored only inside your browser (localStorage). They are not sent to or stored on the operator's servers. If you clear your browser data, they are deleted as well.

Use of sensors

To measure the tilt of your phone, the Game uses your device's orientation sensor. Sensor values are processed only on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Playing online with friends

Playing online with friends uses a technology that connects browsers directly (WebRTC), and the public PeerJS server to help set up the connection. During a game, your name, measured angles and results are sent to the other players in the same room. Because of how WebRTC works, connection information such as your IP address may also be visible to the other players in the room. This information is not stored on the operator's servers. Please do not share room codes with people you do not know.

Ranked Match

When you open Ranked Match, the Game loads Firebase components distributed by Google (from www.gstatic.com) and connects to Firebase Authentication (“anonymous authentication”) and the Firebase Realtime Database. Anonymous authentication does not use an email address or similar information; it only creates a random user ID, which is stored in your browser.

When you play, the following information is stored on Google's Firebase servers: your name, the division (Sensor or Slider), your rating, your numbers of wins, losses and draws, a record of each match (the target, the angle you pressed, the error and the time), and your user ID. Your opponent can see your name, rating and the angle you pressed. The rating ranking shows your name, rank and rating to everyone. Because browsers do not connect directly to each other in Ranked Match, your IP address is not shared with your opponent.

Please do not enter your real name or any name that could identify you. If you would like your Ranked Match records deleted, please contact us at the address above.

Rankings

When you play the Official Apology (once a day), your name, your error in the round, how you played (Sensor or Slider), a randomly generated identifier for your device, and the time of submission are sent to the ranking server (Firebase Realtime Database, provided by Google) and made public to everyone. You can take the challenge once per division per day. No personal information such as an email address is sent. Please do not enter your real name or any name that could identify you. If you would like your entry removed from a ranking, please contact us at the address above.

Advertising

The Game may use the third-party advertising service Google AdSense. Advertising vendors may use cookies to show ads based on your past visits.

  • You can learn how Google uses cookies in advertising in Google's advertising policies.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Ads Settings.

Analytics

To understand how the Game is used (how many people visit, which modes are played, and where visitors come from), the Game uses Google Analytics from Google LLC. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information in a form that does not identify individuals. The collected information is managed according to Google's privacy policy.

  • For details, see the Google Analytics Terms of Service and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • If you do not want your information collected, you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or turn off cookies in your browser.

The name you enter in the Game and your tilt sensor values are not sent to Google Analytics.

Links from Hoshiyomi Daily

If you open this Game from a link on “Hoshiyomi Daily” (hoshiyomi-daily.com), the Game tells Hoshiyomi Daily that you played, once, when you finish a game. The only information sent is the marker that came with the link and the name of the Game. No information that identifies you, such as your name, your records or sensor values, is sent.

Changes

This policy may be changed when necessary without prior notice. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
